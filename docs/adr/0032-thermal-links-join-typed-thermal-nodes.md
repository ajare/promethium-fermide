# Thermal links join typed Thermal nodes

Status: accepted (#853, #855); amends ADR-0028's endpoint scope and duplicate-pair policy.

A Thermal link joins two **Thermal nodes**, not only two Atmosphere compartments. A node exposes a live temperature and finite positive thermal capacity; Atmosphere compartments and Thermal reservoirs qualify. This permits structural reservoir meshes and compartment-to-reservoir conduction without spending the compartment's one heat-only exchanger slot. The link still owns no temperature or capacity: generalizing its endpoints does not turn a zero-capacity connector into a reservoir or reuse the reservoir star solver for a wall.

Endpoint identity is `(kind, id)`, so identically named components of different kinds remain distinct. Kinds are `atmosphere`, `reservoir`, and `machineBody`. A `machineBody` endpoint resolves an Electrolyzer with an authored Thermal body; bodyless machines are refused. For binary and YAML compatibility the historical `aAtmosphereId` / `bAtmosphereId` wire fields retain their positions and names, accompanied by optional `aKind` / `bKind`. Missing kind means atmosphere; atmosphere kinds are omitted when writing, leaving old endpoint records unchanged. Unknown kinds and unresolvable or non-positive-capacity endpoints are refused.

The preserved invariants of ADR-0028 are mandatory: mass-free conduction; the shared closed-form two-body relaxation with reduced heat capacity, equilibrium-first evaluation and `expm1`; Celsius-valid temperature differences; sequential canonical operator splitting keyed by the undirected pair of typed named identities; exact-negation energy booking; signed A-to-B flux and cumulative transfer with Idle/Active reporting, never requested/actual/limiter service fields; and independence from Atmosphere pathways and Station connection throughput or efficiency. Thermal service class remains a machine service contract, not a restriction on passive conduction.

The pass still precedes the compartment vapour/thermal integration so conducted heat participates in same-tick condensation. Both atmosphere and reservoir provisional temperatures include already-booked star-solve exchange (#833). Reservoir conduction is booked separately from exchanger heat and enters reservoir integration exactly once. Every endpoint's cumulative conducted term participates in its energy residual and the per-tick conservation audit. Machine bodies seed the pass from their live temperature after waste charge and sink-leg integration (#855), and integrate only the additional conducted energy. A body may cool solely through links, or have no heat path and self-limit through its temperature gate; configure records a warning event for the latter. Sink-leg qualification and connection losses remain service semantics and never constrain a Thermal link.

#853 additionally refuses duplicate undirected endpoint pairs as well as self-links. This supersedes the earlier authoring allowance for multiple parallel links: represent parallel structural contacts by summing their conductances on one link. The underlying closed form remains additive under composition; the former parallel smoke fixtures now verify that composition through equivalent conductance-duration products and assert duplicate refusal. Existing single-pair atmosphere documents retain their wire representation and behaviour; older documents containing duplicate pairs must consolidate those pairs to satisfy the new validation rule.
