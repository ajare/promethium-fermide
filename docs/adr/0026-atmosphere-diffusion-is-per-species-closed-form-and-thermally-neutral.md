# Atmosphere diffusion is per-species, closed-form, and thermally neutral

Status: accepted

Atmosphere compartments were isolated by construction: gas left a compartment only by leaking to the environment or by a scheduled airlock cycle, and no two compartments ever mixed. Authoring a connected station requires gas to move between volumes, and the shape of that movement decides what the model can express at all. Three decisions were taken together because each one constrains the others.

**Diffusion is per-species, not bulk flow.** Each gas moves down its own partial-pressure gradient independently, so oxygen can cross a pathway one way while carbon dioxide crosses the other, and the attractor is uniform partial pressures across everything the pathways join. The rejected alternative was total-pressure bulk advection, where the whole mixture moves down the total gradient and species move proportionally to the upstream composition. Bulk flow is what a fan-driven duct does, but it freezes composition the instant pressures balance: two compartments at equal total pressure and different composition would never mix, so a closed compartment's carbon dioxide could only ever be cleared by a fan, never by the opening itself. Per-species diffusion is the reading in which the abstraction does something at balanced pressure, and it keeps each gas's ledger closing on its own, which is how compartments are already accounted.

**The exchange integrates in closed form, not by explicit Euler.** In the density-like variable `a = m·T_K / V` a pathway pair is linear — `d(a−b)/dt = −(α+β)(a−b)` — and is solved exactly across the tick rather than stepped. This was a stability decision, not a taste for elegance. A wide pathway between small compartments has a relaxation time shorter than the 100 ms station step; explicit Euler overshoots and oscillates, and every tick that the overshoot is clamped back to the donor's inventory the clamp silently substitutes bookkeeping for physics while the mass ledger still closes perfectly — nothing would have reported the loss. The closed form cannot go negative while both ends start non-negative, cannot oscillate, needs no clamping, and is nearly independent of step size, which is exactly the property the path-equivalence stability tests probe. It also removes any need for a maximum pathway area: an absurd area between tiny volumes simply lands on its equilibrium in one tick, monotonically. Stepping explicitly would have forced an authored ceiling on area to keep the solver alive, and that ceiling would have been a stability artefact posing as a design parameter.

**The exchange is thermally neutral within the tick.** Temperature is evaluated at tick start and held across the step, so the coefficients stay constant and the closed form remains the exact solution of the stated law. This is why neutrality is not merely a scope cut: if the moving mass carried enthalpy, temperature would change during the tick, the coefficients would vary with it, and the exact solution would no longer be exact. The accepted limits are stated rather than hidden — a pathway never equalizes temperature, so two compartments at the same composition and different temperatures reach a mass-ratio equilibrium and keep their temperature difference indefinitely; and no latent heat crosses a pathway, so vapour diffusing into a cold compartment condenses there against that compartment's own saturation solve and gives up its condensation heat locally. Heat moves only through a compartment's heat-only exchanger to a thermal reservoir, which is already the mechanism for making a compartment warm or cold.

**Exterior vacuum boundary (amendment).** A pathway may connect a compartment to fixed zero partial pressures instead of another contained inventory (ADR-0027). For each gas the finite-side equation is `dm/dt = -D_i * area * openness * T_K/V * m`, integrated as `m(t+dt) = m(t) * exp(-D_i * area * openness * T_K/V * dt)`. This is the zero-pressure boundary limit of the existing simplified law, not bulk compressible or choked decompression. The exterior has no temperature to evaluate, never fills, and never returns gas. Openness remains linear in conductance (not in the finite-interval mass dose). Zero openness blocks all loss; enormous area safely empties the compartment without clamping. Temperature remains unchanged by the pathway itself; no expansion cooling or enthalpy transport is claimed. Every removed species is accounted as external loss, distinct from internal exchange, constant leaks and airlock cycles.

Supporting decisions that hang off the three above:

- **The per-gas diffusion coefficient is a global constant in code, never an authored per-pathway value**, so two pathways with identical area and openness always behave identically and the station's overall mixing speed is a one-line change. Because the law carries no length scale, that coefficient is an *effective turbulent* coefficient standing in for the path length the abstraction does not have, calibrated so a 1 m² fully-open pathway between two 100 m³ compartments substantially equalizes in the low single-digit minutes of simulated time. It must not be "corrected" to the real molecular constant, which is orders of magnitude slower and would make every pathway indistinguishable from absent.
- **Partial pressure derives from absolute temperature**, as it already does, so the density variable is in kelvin. Implementing it in Celsius would make a compartment at 0 °C report zero partial pressure and diffuse as though it were empty.
- **Pathways resolve sequentially in fixed declaration order** rather than as a coupled matrix. This is operator splitting — first-order and small at 10 Hz — chosen because non-negativity survives the pass regardless of order, and because it keeps every flow attributable to one named pathway in the ledger. A simultaneous solve would be more exactly coupled while making that attribution invisible.
- **The diffusion pass runs after crew metabolism and before the per-compartment loop.** It is the only placement where vapour that just arrived meets the destination's saturation solve in the same tick, preserving the standing guarantee that a published compartment is never supersaturated against its own state; after the loop it would violate that invariant for a tick while closing the ledger perfectly, and the coefficients would be evaluated at end-of-tick rather than tick-start values.

Reversing any of the three is expensive and changes the model rather than its implementation. Bulk flow instead of per-species diffusion means composition stops mixing whenever pressures balance and the per-gas ledger collapses into one mixture flow. Explicit Euler instead of the closed form reintroduces stiffness, clamping, and step-size sensitivity, and would need an area ceiling and a re-examination of every stability assertion. Carrying enthalpy instead of neutrality breaks the constant-coefficient premise and needs a different integrator and a three-way mass/pressure/temperature coupling. That is why these are recorded together here rather than left implicit in the diff.

**Heat is a separate aggregate, not a pathway property (amendment, #793/#794).** The neutrality stated above leaves a hole with nothing on the other side of it: a shared bulkhead that conducts, a greenhouse warming its neighbour through a common wall, a cold surface drawing heat out of an adjacent compartment. That hole is filled by the **Thermal link** — an explicitly authored, undirected conduction relationship between two Atmosphere compartments carrying a declared conductance in kW/K, integrated in closed form per link (`k = 1 - exp(-G·(1/Ca + 1/Cb)·dt)`, `Q = Ca·Cb/(Ca + Cb)·(Ta - Tb)·k`) against tick-start temperatures before the per-compartment loop. This is not a reversal of the neutrality decision: the pathway remains thermally neutral and moves mass, the link moves heat and no mass, and the two aggregates are deliberately independent so a wall and a hole stay separately authorable. Neutrality held because the pathway's coefficients must not move with temperature; the link needs no such restraint because two-body conduction is linear with temperature-independent coefficients in the first place, which is exactly why heat could not ride the pathway but can ride its own aggregate. A compartment with no link still never shares heat, so the no-implicit-exchange rule keeps holding with a second named, author-controlled crossing alongside the pathway.
